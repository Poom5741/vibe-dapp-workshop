# Requirements Brief R1 — Collection Identity
> From: your facilitator (acting as the client) · For: the Art station
> YOUR job: turn this into a spec your agent can build from (speckit.specify)

## The client wants

A small NFT collection with a real identity — not "10 random cool images."

**Deliverables:**
- Collection **name** + one-line **tagline**
- 3 NFT artworks, square 1:1, consistent style (they must look like ONE collection)
- 1 hero banner, 16:9
- All saved into `app/public/art/`

## Constraints

- Theme must be YOURS: your major, your club, your city, your humor. Not "generic crypto."
- One style sentence reused across all four images — that's what makes it a collection.
- Filenames the app expects: `art1`, `art2`, `art3`, `hero` (the starter files are `.svg`;
  keep your base names and tell your agent the extension).

## Open questions — the client will NOT decide these for you

- What is the collection about? Who is it for?
- Visual style + palette? (The workshop default is neon purple/red on dark — you may keep
  it, or reject it and own the look.)
- Composition: what's in the frame, where does the banner title go?

→ Write your answers as **§2 What it looks like** and **§4 What to leave out** of your
spec in `speckit/specs/art-spec.md`.
